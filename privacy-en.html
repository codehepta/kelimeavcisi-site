Kelime Avcısı — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Kelime Avcısı is a Turkish and English word learning game for ages 6–10. The App Store build works offline. It sends no data to the developer and contains no advertising, tracking, analytics services, accounts or in-app purchases.

Information stored on the device
The nickname, character, learning and interface languages, completed levels and words, stars and scores are stored locally. Difficult letters, daily quests, badges and the last 60 days of activity help parents understand progress. Sound, motion, text size and readability preferences are also stored locally. A real name is not required.

Sharing and device backups
Copying the summary on the Statistics screen writes it to the system clipboard only after a parental check. This is optional; the game does not send the summary itself. If you paste it into another app, that app's privacy rules apply. Operating system clipboard synchronization and device backups may transfer information to your other devices or backups according to your device settings. The developer has no access to those backups.

Speech and permissions
The game reads its built-in words using the device's system speech service. It does not listen to the microphone or record audio. Speech can be disabled in settings. Downloading system voices and configuring the speech service are controlled by the operating system. The game does not request camera, microphone, photo, contact or location access. It uses no advertising identifier and does not request tracking permission.

Retention and deletion
Local progress is retained while the app remains in use; daily activity details are limited to 60 days. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app may keep it. You can manage system backups and clipboard contents separately in device settings. The developer holds no copy of this information.

Children's privacy
The game does not collect children's information on a server or sell it to third parties. There is no chat, social network, external advertising or purchasing. Contacting support by email is optional; we use your email address and message only to respond to your request. Please do not include children's private information in support messages.
